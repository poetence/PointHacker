What we heard

The brief

A web app that shows people the highest-value way to use their credit-card points: transfer partners vs. portal, by destination, goal and promo. The mark should make the gain obvious, a constant rise, in the spirit of The Points Guy, using at most the P from the name.

savvyrisingconfidenttravel-ready

Success: reads as gain and direction at a glance, uses only the P, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour, sits on the site's emerald accent.

Final design

Heading Up

A bold P with a navigation arrow cut from its bowl, aimed up and to the right: your points heading somewhere better.

  • The arrow points to 45 degrees, the classic up-and-to-the-right of gain, and is also the travel-app 'navigate' arrow
  • Uses only the P; the wordmark is Space Grotesk 600, the site's own heading face, outlined
  • A dedicated small cut with a larger arrow keeps the idea alive at 16 px
  • One colour: emerald-600, the site's single accent, so the mark and the UI speak with one voice
